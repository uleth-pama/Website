News overview
The Physics, Astronomy, & Mathematics Association (PAMA) is a non-profit student organization and modern successor to the historic Physics Club. We aim to equip students with the necessary skills and resources to succeed as future scientists.

PAMA serves all full-time and part-time university students, faculty, staff, and local community members. We foster a safe, inclusive environment for students to learn and grow.
